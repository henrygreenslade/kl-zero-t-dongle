KLDongle flasher

Firmware 1.3.0 for the LILYGO T-Dongle-S3 · Kaffelogic Nano 7 (older board)

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the browser talks to the dongle over USB).
  2. Hold the button on the dongle while you plug it into the computer, then let go. It is now in download mode.
  3. Click Install, choose the port (on a Mac usbmodem…, on Windows COM…) and confirm.
  4. When it says done, unplug the dongle and plug it in again without the button.
This browser cannot flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.

Then copy your Kaffelogic stick's files onto the dongle (its internal storage, or a FAT32 microSD card) and add wifi_config.txt; see the README.

Updating only replaces the program: your profiles, logs and WiFi settings (on the SD card or in the dongle's internal storage) are kept. Unofficial project, not affiliated with Kaffelogic.
